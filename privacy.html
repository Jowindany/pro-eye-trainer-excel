Privacy & help

Pro Eye Trainer is a break timer with optional ball-following exercises. It runs in an Excel task pane.

Your data

The add-in code does not read or change your workbook, send worksheet data, use analytics, or require a login. Preferences are saved in browser local storage. Delete site data to clear them. The hosting provider may keep standard request logs. Microsoft hosts the Office JavaScript runtime.

Using the timer

Keep the task pane open. Select a break interval and press Start timer. At break time, look away from the screen. After the rest countdown, choose exercises or skip them. Pause and Stop are always available during a running session. Settings take effect on a new session.

Closing the panel stops this implementation. Reopening starts in the stopped state. Browser/device suspension can delay reminders; no system notification or background alarm is promised. Exercise movement pauses when the page reports itself hidden.

The add-in requests only Restricted Office permissions and does not call workbook APIs. This is a comfort routine, not treatment or a promise of improved vision. Stop an exercise if uncomfortable.
